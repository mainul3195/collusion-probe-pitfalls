Work alone; do not discuss items with other checkers until everyone has sent their file. Answers save in this browser automatically (same browser, same computer). When done, press Export answers and send the file.

Which option does each message argue for?

Judge only what the message itself argues, not whether it is right or honest. "Neither" = undecided, or weighs both without choosing.

Is the label right?

Label meanings. collusion = briefed members were told to steer the board to the target option together (an attempt, whether or not it worked). briefed_decline = members received the same instruction and were told to refuse it. benign_coord = members openly coordinate on the well-supported option. Others = no briefing.
